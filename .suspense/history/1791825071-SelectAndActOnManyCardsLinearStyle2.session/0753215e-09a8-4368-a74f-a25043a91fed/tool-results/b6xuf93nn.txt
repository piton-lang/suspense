# Planning

## How To Build

A Planning should be built as a reusable component that will be reused throughout the rest of the codebase.  A component is configurable through arguments or properties that alter its behaviour, appearance, and details.  A Planning should encapsulate all of its own reusable behaviour, while leaving the wiring, integration, and integration to the domain of the application.

## Theme

Drawn in the [Theme](../../ui/theme/index.md#theme): its surfaces, text, lines, accent, and status colours are the theme's roles, named as the theme names them, in both dark and light mode, never colours of its own.

## Pitch

A plain, quick list of the work still to do, as Linear keeps issues: stories written ahead of time, each saying how it will be sent, Code, Chain, Spec, Ask, or Freeform, gathered into the next sprint and sent to the agent in order, all at once.

## Description

Planning opens in a tab of its own in the body, "Planning", with a checklist icon, as a file opens, as the [Body](../application/Body.md#body) says, from the ribbon's Planning tab or the palette's "Planning". It is a kanban board, filling the body beneath the tab bar, as the [Body](../application/Body.md#body) places it, from the tab bar down to the chat input and from the body's left edge to its right. Its sections sit side by side as columns, in the order work moves, left to right: "Backlog", "Next sprint", "Current sprint", and "Done", then "Findings", the last, as research says. Each column is headed by its name and count, with its actions, as the layout says, and holds its stories as cards, one beneath another. Every column is always there, whatever it holds; one with nothing in it shows its empty state, as the states say.

## Stories

A story is one piece of work, written as it will be sent: a title, a prompt, and how it will be sent. Each is a story card in its column, as the parts say, quiet and dense, as Linear keeps an issue, showing all a story shows: a drag grip at its top left; its title beside it, the card's one primary thing, in the body's text colour, wrapping onto a second line and cut off with an ellipsis only after that; and beneath the title, a single muted property row of small

### Items

a small mode picker, a pill reading its mode, "Code", "Chain",

"Spec", "Ask", or "Freeform", in that mode's colour, as the [PromptModes](../prompt-mode/modes/index.md#prompt-modes) colours it; its step count and a small icon for anything attached, when it has them; and, once it has been sent, its status, as a task's is shown, at the row's right. Hovering a card shows a menu button at its top right. A story has no Send of its own, as the sprint says.
A column is too narrow to open a story in place, so clicking a card never expands it beneath itself or moves the cards below it: it opens the story in the side panel, docked at the board's right, as the layout places it and the parts draw a panel, headed by the story's title. The panel holds all an open story holds: its title, editable in place; its prompt, in a [GrowingTextInput](../../ui/components/growing-text-input/index.md#growing-text-input) with the chat input's highlighting, completions, and slicing, as the [ChatInput](../prompt-mode/chat-input/index.md#chat-input) has, and its attachments beneath; its parameters, its mode, model, effort, and Slice, as the chat input shows them; its steps, with Add step, as its steps say; and its Spec citations, as the spec grounding says. Every change is saved as it is made. While its story is open, its card stays highlighted, its line in the theme's accent, so which story the panel shows is always plain. Only one story is open at a time: clicking another card opens that one in the panel instead. Escape, the panel's close button, or clicking the open story's card again closes it, and its card's highlight with it.

## Creating

Creating a story is quick, as adding a card is in Trello and adding an issue is in Linear: a story is added in the list it belongs in, right where it will sit. The Backlog and Next sprint each end with an "Add story" line at their foot, beneath their last card, or beneath their empty state when they have none. Clicking it opens a composer in its place: a blank story card, drawn as the parts draw one, whose title is a text box, focused, in the mode last picked for a story, with nothing else to fill in. Typing a title and pressing Enter adds the story to that list, where the composer was, and opens another composer beneath it, so a list can be written down without the mouse. Enter with the title empty adds nothing. Escape discards the composer, and whatever was typed in it, and the "Add story" line comes back; clicking away while its title is empty discards it too, and clicking away with a title typed adds the story, as Enter does, but opens no other composer. A story added this way is not opened in the side panel; its card is there to be clicked, as the stories say, once more than its title is wanted.
Hovering the Backlog's or Next sprint's heading shows a labelled "+ Add" button in it, as the layout places it, which opens the composer at the top of that list, above its first card, so a story can go first without being dragged there; Enter adds it there and opens the next composer beneath it, so stories written one after another keep the order they were written in. Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on macOS) while Planning has focus opens the composer at the foot of the list that has focus, the Backlog or Next sprint, as the keyboard says, and at the foot of Next sprint when neither has it. Only one composer is open at a time: opening another discards an empty one and adds one with a title, as clicking away does. Current sprint, Done, and Findings have no "Add story" line, no "+ Add", and no composer, and Ctrl+N never adds a story to them.
A story goes into the list it was added to, then: the Backlog or Next sprint, whichever composer it was written in. A duplicate goes into its original's list, just beneath it, and a story added from an Ask answer's card with "Add to sprint" goes into Next sprint, at its end. An accepted proposal for a new story, from drafting, research, or an Ask answer's proposal card, still goes into the Backlog, at its end, as the proposal cards say, and reaches a sprint only once it is moved to Next sprint. A story can always be moved between the two afterwards, as the sprint says. Adding a story never starts a sprint by itself, except where Start automatically starts one when a story is added to an empty Next sprint, as auto start says; a story added to the Backlog never starts one. A title with no prompt sends its title as its prompt.
A story can also be prompted into existence: "Draft stories", a button beside the Backlog's heading, opens a small box to say what is wanted, in a sentence or a paragraph, and asks the harness, in a run of its own with no session, with the planning prompt of the [BakedSystemPrompts](../harness-integration/baked-prompts/index.md#baked-system-prompts), to break it into stories, each with a title, a prompt, and the mode that fits it. They come back as proposals, as the drafting and proposals say, each to be accepted, edited, or rejected, and none reaches the plan until it is accepted. An Ask answer's prompt cards also offer "Add to sprint", which adds the card's prompt as a story in its mode to Next sprint.

## Sprint

Stories move between the Backlog and Next sprint by dragging, as the [DragReorderBehaviour](../../ui/behaviours/drag-reorder/index.md#drag-reorder-behaviour) says, a card dragged by its grip from one column into a gap in the other as within either, the gap opened in the column the pointer is over, as motion says; an empty one takes a drop at its top. Dragged near the board's left or right edge, the board scrolls sideways, and near a column's top or bottom, that column scrolls, as dragging says. Only the Backlog and Next sprint take drops: Current sprint and Done open no gap, a story dropped on them stays where it was, and their cards can't be picked up, nor can a finding. Stories move too from a card's menu, "Move to next sprint" or "Move to backlog". Their order within Next sprint is the order they will be sent. A card's menu also has "Duplicate" and "Delete".
"Send sprint", a primary button at the right of Next sprint's heading, enabled while it holds a story, sends every story in it, in order, as though each had been written in the chat input on its mode's tab and sent, one after another, as the [PromptSending](../prompt-mode/sending/index.md#prompt-sending) says: each task queues in its lane behind the one before it, in the order of the sprint, and each question runs at once. The stories go into the queue as one block, so nothing sent later comes between them, and the queue's auto-send carries them on as ever, as the [QueueList](../prompt-mode/queue-list/index.md#queue-list) says.
Each story starts a conversation of its own, as though New conversation had been pressed just before it, and so does each later step of a story's chain: a story never carries on the conversation of the story before it, nor of anything its lane ran before the sprint. New conversation, pressed before the sprint was sent, is left for the next prompt written in the chat input.
A story can't be sent on its own. The only way a story reaches work is through the sprint: a story in the Backlog waits until it is moved into Next sprint and the sprint is sent, and a story in Next sprint goes when the sprint does. Nothing in Planning, its cards, their menus, the open story, the keyboard, the palette, or the ribbon, sends a single story, and nothing elsewhere sends a story by any other route: a story can't be dragged or copied into the chat input or the queue to be sent from there, and its menu has no "Send" or "Edit in chat input". The chat input's own prompts are not stories and are sent as ever; once a prompt is a story, the sprint is its only way to work. To send one story at once, it is the only story in Next sprint when Send sprint is pressed. A story that failed or was cancelled is sent again the same way, with the next sprint.
Sending the sprint moves its stories into the "Current sprint" column, to the right of Next sprint, so Next sprint is empty again and stories added meanwhile go there, to be the sprint after. Current sprint and Done are columns of the board whatever they hold, never appearing, disappearing, or collapsing; while no sprint is under way Current sprint is empty and shows its empty state, as Done does before anything is done. A story in Current sprint keeps its place, its card showing its task's status as it queues, runs, and ends, and clicking it opens its task in the Tasks tab; it can't be moved or edited there. Once every story of Current sprint has ended, its column's heading offers "Close sprint", which moves the stories that are done into the Done column, their cards muted, and those that failed, were cancelled, or stopped to the start of Next sprint, in their order, to be sent again. While a sprint is current, Send sprint is disabled, its tooltip saying a sprint is already under way.
Send sprint only ever moves into Current sprint the stories it actually sent. A story with nothing to send, its title and its prompt both empty, is skipped: it stays in Next sprint, where it was, and Send sprint's result says, muted, how many it skipped and why, as "1 empty story left in Next sprint". A sprint where every story is empty sends nothing and makes no Current sprint.
Once a sprint is done, it can always be closed. Every story of Current sprint counts as ended once its task has ended, done, failed, cancelled, or stopped, or once its task can no longer be found, as when its record was deleted, it was never recorded, or the application quit before it was. A story whose task is missing is never shown as Queued, or as anything still to come: its card reads "Task missing", muted, in the theme's warning colour, its tooltip saying its task can't be found, and it counts as ended and not Done. A story still waiting in its lane's queue, or held for review, as the paused section says, is still to come and hasn't ended. So Close sprint shows as soon as no story of Current sprint is building, compiling, running, paused, queued, or held, and never waits on a task that can't arrive.
Close sprint treats a story whose task is missing as it treats one that failed or was cancelled: it goes back to the start of Next sprint, with the others sent again, in their order, never into Done. Closing saves the sections, Done, Next sprint, the Backlog, and the emptied Current sprint, to `order.json` straight away, as the [ProjectData](../project-data/index.md#project-data) says, and Current sprint's state is worked out afresh from the stories' tasks whenever the plan is read, so a sprint that finished, or whose tasks went missing, before a reload or a restart can still be closed after it, as before.

## Motion

Dragging a card on the board feels as it does in Trello, and the board opens the gap, as the [DragReorderBehaviour](../../ui/behaviours/drag-reorder/index.md#drag-reorder-behaviour) lets its user do, in place of the accent line alone. Only the board's cards move this way; steps in an open story, and every other list, are dragged as the [DragReorderBehaviour](../../ui/behaviours/drag-reorder/index.md#drag-reorder-behaviour) says, with its line.
A card picked up by its grip lifts off the board as a full-size copy of itself, the same width and height, drawn as the parts draw a story card, tilted a few degrees, about 3, on the theme's overlay surface with its edge border and the soft shadow the [Theme](../../ui/theme/index.md#theme)'s elevation gives floating surfaces, the one shadow on the board. It follows the pointer, held where it was grabbed, and never takes the mouse. The card's own place in its column becomes the gap it left, empty, the card's height, so the column doesn't jump as it lifts.
Where the card would land, the cards slide apart to open a placeholder the dragged card's height, 8 pixels from the cards either side, as the cards are spaced: a box the card's size on the theme's well surface, with its corners rounded 6 pixels as a card's are, and no border, so it reads as an empty slot. The gap is chosen as the [DragReorderBehaviour](../../ui/behaviours/drag-reorder/index.md#drag-reorder-behaviour) chooses it, among the cards of the column the pointer is over, and only one is open at a time: as the pointer moves into another gap, or another column, the gap it left closes as the new one opens, and the gap the card came from closes once the pointer leaves its column, opening again if it comes back. A column that takes no drops opens no gap, and its cards never move while a card is dragged over it.
Nothing waits on motion while the user is waiting on it. The gap the card would drop into is updated at once, the moment the pointer crosses the middle of a card, exactly as the [DragReorderBehaviour](../../ui/behaviours/drag-reorder/index.md#drag-reorder-behaviour) says, and a drop always goes where the gap was chosen, even while the cards are still sliding toward it; only the sliding of the cards around it is animated, quickly, as below.
Dropped over an open gap, the copy settles into it: it straightens, its shadow fades, and it moves from where it was released into the placeholder, which it then is, the story saved in its new place at the moment of the drop, as every change to the plan is. Dropped anywhere else, or where it began, it settles back into the gap it came from the same way, and nothing changes. Escape while dragging cancels it, settling the card back where it was.
A card moved any other way animates to its new place, in Linear's

### Style

a quick motion of about 150 milliseconds, eased out, sliding

## Layout

Everything in Planning is laid out on one rhythm, so the board scans at a glance. The board fills the body beneath the tab bar, with 24 pixels of padding at its top, sides, and foot. Its columns are all the same fixed width, 320 pixels, 16 pixels apart, each as tall as the board, set side by side from its left edge in the order work moves, Backlog, Next sprint, Current sprint, Done, and Findings, the last. Each column is a list, as Trello draws one: a container of its own, as the parts draw a list, on the theme's recessed surface, one step from the base the board is on, its corners rounded 8 pixels, with its heading and its cards inside it; its surface and the gaps between columns set it apart, and it has no border.
Each column's heading reads as Linear heads a group: a row 32 pixels tall at the list's top, holding, from its left, the column's status icon, 14 pixels, as the parts give it; its name in small semibold text, in the theme's primary text colour; its count beside it, muted; and its actions at its right, each a labelled button, never an icon alone, as the sections give them: "Draft stories" and "Research" on the Backlog; "Send sprint" and "Start automatically" on Next sprint; "Close sprint" on Current sprint, once its sprint can close. A heading with more actions than fit its width wraps them onto a second line beneath its name rather than cutting any off. On the Backlog and Next sprint, hovering the heading, or focus anywhere in it, shows "+ Add" just after its count, a small ghost button with a plus icon and its label, never the icon alone, its tooltip saying it adds a story at the top of the list; it takes its room in the heading whether shown or not, so the heading's actions never shift as it appears, and the other columns' headings have none. 8 pixels beneath the heading come its cards, one beneath another, 8 pixels apart, each the list's full inner width.
At the foot of the Backlog and of Next sprint, 8 pixels beneath its last card, or beneath its empty state, is its "Add story" line: a row 32 pixels tall, the list's full inner width, with a plus icon and the label "Add story" in small muted text, its corners rounded 6 pixels, and no border; hovered, it takes the theme's hover surface and its text the theme's primary text colour. It scrolls with the cards, after the last of them. Its composer, as creating says, is drawn where it was clicked, at the foot in the line's place, or at the top, above the first card, from "+ Add": a story card, as the parts draw one, its title a text box in the card's title text, with the focus ring around the card, and beneath it, muted, "Enter to add, Escape to cancel"; the cards below it move down to make room, and the list scrolls to keep it in view. Current sprint, Done, and Findings have no "Add story" line.
Each column scrolls up and down on its own, its heading staying at its top while its cards scroll beneath it, inside the list's rounded corners, with the [Scrollbar](../../ui/components/scrollbar/index.md#scrollbar) shown along the list's right, inside it, while it holds more than fits. The board scrolls sideways when its columns are wider than the tab, with the [Scrollbar](../../ui/components/scrollbar/index.md#scrollbar) along its foot; while they fit, it doesn't scroll. Every card's state reads the same way, as below, and every label is the same small text, muted where it is secondary.
The side panel is the one place anything opens: an open story, Draft stories, and Research, never beneath a heading or a card, and never in a popup or over the board. A composer is not an opened story but a card being written, and is the one thing drawn in a list, where its story will sit. It is docked at the right of Planning's tab, 400 pixels wide, as tall as the tab, from the tab bar down to the chat input, a 1 pixel line in the theme's line colour along its left edge, and it scrolls up and down on its own, its heading staying at its top, with the [Scrollbar](../../ui/components/scrollbar/index.md#scrollbar) along its right while it holds more than fits. Only one thing is in it at a time: opening a story, drafting, or research replaces whatever it held, and closing it leaves nothing there. While it is open the board narrows to the space left of it, its columns keeping their width and scrolling sideways as ever when they no longer fit; the panel never covers the board, and the board never covers it. Closed, it takes no space and the board fills the tab again. It slides in and out at the right, as motion says.
While paused, the pause bar runs across the top of the whole board, above every column, as the paused section says, beside the side panel when it is open, never over it. Each column is headed as above, so nothing sits outside the rhythm, and an open story, a drafting or research panel, and the citations and steps inside a story all use the same five parts, below.

## Parts

Everything in Planning is built from five parts, each always drawn the same way, in the theme's roles, named as the theme names them, in both dark and light mode:
- A list: one per column, as the layout places it, 320 pixels wide and as tall as the board, on the theme's recessed surface, one step from the base, with no border, its corners rounded 8 pixels, 8 pixels of padding; its heading row at its top, as the layout says, then its cards, or its empty state centred beneath its heading; its heading's status icon one per column, each always the same: the Backlog a dashed circle in the theme's tertiary text colour; Next sprint an empty circle in the theme's secondary text colour; Current sprint a half-filled circle in the theme's accent; Done a filled circle with a check in the theme's success colour; and Findings a magnifying glass in the theme's secondary text colour; each only repeating the column's name, never standing for it
- A story card: a box the full inner width of its list, on the theme's raised surface, with a 1 pixel line in the theme's line colour, corners rounded 6 pixels, 8 pixels of padding above and below and 10 at its sides, 8 pixels from the next; a top line with its drag grip, 16 pixels, in the theme's tertiary text colour, at its left, its title beside it, the card's primary text, in up to two lines, cut off with an ellipsis after the second, and its menu button at its right on hover and always while it is focused; beneath the title, 4 pixels below it, a single property row, 20 pixels tall, in small muted text, of small items 8 pixels apart: its mode pill, in its mode's colour, as the PromptModes colours it, its step count, its attachment icon, each when it has them, and its status and time, right-aligned, once it has been sent; and, in Current sprint, its steps' lines beneath, as its steps say; used for every story, in every column, muted in Done. Its states are three distinct steps, as the Theme's hierarchy orders them: hovering raises it one surface step, to the theme's hover surface; the open story keeps a 3 pixel line in the theme's accent along its left edge, as the stories say, whether or not it is hovered; a selected card, as the selection below says, takes the theme's selected surface in place of its raised one, hovered or not; and focus shows the theme's focus ring, in the accent, 2 pixels around it, over any of them, so a hover is always quieter than a selection, and a selection than focus, as the Theme orders them, and the open story's line, an edge rather than a surface, reads apart from both, a card being open, selected, and focused at once without any of the three hiding another
- A row: 36 pixels tall, for steps in an open story and for findings; its secondary text muted and cut off with an ellipsis, its buttons small ghost buttons with labels, shown at its right on hover and always while it is focused
- A panel: the side panel, docked at the board's right, full height, as the layout places it, on the theme's raised surface, its corners square, 12 pixels of padding, a heading row of 32 pixels with its name, as "Draft stories", "Research", or the story's title, and its close button, its content beneath, and its actions in a footer row, right-aligned, the primary action last; used for an open story, drafting, and research
- A card: a box inside a panel, on the theme's base surface, with a 1 pixel line in the theme's line colour, corners rounded 4 pixels, 12 pixels of padding, 8 pixels from the next; a label row at its top, its content, and a footer row of labelled buttons; used for proposals and for each finding's summary
Labels are words, never icons alone: every button reads what it does, "Accept", "Send sprint", "Start automatically", and an icon beside a label only repeats it. Each heading's count is a plain number, muted, and each status a coloured label, as the states say. The pause bar is a row across the whole board's top, above its columns, 40 pixels tall, its text and buttons on one line, the same height whatever it says.
Stories are selected as Linear selects issues, to be acted on together. Only a story in the Backlog or Next sprint can be selected, the two lists that take moves, as the sprint says; a card in Current sprint or Done, and a finding, never is. Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on macOS) on a card toggles it alone in or out of the selection, the rest kept; Shift+click selects the range within its column from the card last clicked or toggled there to the clicked one, inclusive, added to the selection, or the clicked card alone when nothing in that column was clicked or toggled yet; and X toggles one from the keyboard, as the keyboard says. A selection can hold stories from both lists at once. A plain click opens the story in the side panel as ever, and leaves the selection as it is. Selected cards show the theme's selected surface, as the story card above says, distinct from focus and from the open story's line. The selection is the board's own, never saved with the plan: a selected story leaves it when it is deleted or leaves the Backlog and Next sprint, as when the sprint it is in is sent.
While any card is selected, the bulk bar runs across the board's foot, beneath its columns, beside the side panel when it is open, never over it nor over the board: a row 40 pixels tall, on the theme's raised surface with a 1 pixel line in the theme's line colour along its top, the columns shortening to make room as it slides up in motion's quick motion, and lengthening again as it slides away once nothing is selected. At its left it names how many are selected, as "3 selected"; at its right, labelled buttons, never icons alone, in this order: "Move to next sprint", which moves the selected stories in the Backlog to the end of Next sprint; "Move to backlog", which moves those in Next sprint to the end of the Backlog; "Change mode", which opens a mode picker above it, the one a card's pill opens, setting the mode of every selected story to the one picked, their steps keeping their own; "Duplicate", which duplicates each, every copy just beneath its original, as a card's menu does; "Delete", which deletes them all after one confirmation naming how many, as "Delete 3 stories?"; and "Clear", which empties the selection. Every move keeps the stories' order, as the selection orders them, the Backlog's first, top to bottom, then Next sprint's, and animates as motion says; a move button is disabled, its tooltip saying why, while none of the selection is in the list it moves from. Moved stories stay selected, duplicates are not selected, and the originals stay so. The bar has no Send and sends nothing: a story still reaches work only through the sprint, as the sprint says.
Dragging a selected card by its grip carries the whole selection, in its order: the other selected cards leave their places as it lifts, their columns closing up, and the lifted copy is the grabbed card, as motion draws it, with the cards it carries stacked a few pixels behind it and their count beside its title. The gap opens as tall as the cards together, 8 pixels apart, and dropped there they settle into it in their order, all in that list, saved at once; dropped anywhere else, or cancelled with Escape, they all settle back where they were. Dragging a card that isn't selected moves it alone and leaves the selection as it is.
Right-clicking a card opens its menu at the pointer, the same menu its hover button opens, with the same items, acting on that card alone, in place of the browser's own; it closes as that menu does, Escape among the ways.

## States

Each part of Planning has the same few states, shown the same way wherever they come up, so a state is never guessed from what is missing.
- Empty: a single muted line, centred in the column beneath its heading, saying what is missing and what to do, as "Nothing planned. Drag stories here, or press Ctrl+N.", with no illustration
- Loading: a spinner and a short muted label of what is happening, as "Drafting stories…", in the place the result will appear, never a blank space
- Paused: a story whose task is paused shows "Paused" as its status, in the theme's warning colour, with a small pause icon, and a "Resume" button on its card, as the PromptSending says of pausing; a sprint with any story paused says so in its heading, as "1 paused", in the same colour
- Error: a line in the theme's error colour, saying what went wrong in plain words, with what the harness or the application reported beneath it collapsed under "Details", and a "Try again" button where trying again can help
A story's status on its card is the same coloured label a task has, as the [MessageList](../prompt-mode/message-list/index.md#message-list)'s status says, Queued, Running with its spinner and time, Done, Failed, Cancelled, Paused, or, where its task can't be found, Task missing, as the sprint says, and a story not yet sent shows nothing there, so its absence means "not sent".
Each part shows each state that can come up for it, worded for it:
- Story list: empty, "Nothing planned. Press Ctrl+N to add a story." in Next sprint, "Nothing waiting." in the Backlog, "No sprint under way. Send sprint to start one." in Current sprint, and "Nothing done yet." in Done; loading, while the plan is read on opening, a few skeleton cards in each column, never an empty column; error, when the plan can't be read or saved, saying which file and why, with "Try again"
- Research and its findings: loading, the live line of what it is reading with its spinner; empty, "No findings yet." in Findings; error, the run's failure with "Try again", which keeps the question in its box; a finding that ended early, as stopped, marked "Incomplete", muted, in its row
- Pause: the pause bar, in the theme's warning colour, as the paused section says, and the sprint's Send buttons disabled with their tooltips saying why; a sprint that can't start for a failed story says so in its heading, in the same colour
- Proposals: loading, the drafting line and spinner; empty, "No stories proposed." with "Draft again"; error, the run's failure with "Try again"; a proposal with no spec cited, in the warning colour with "Draft again", as the spec grounding says

## Keyboard

Everything in Planning can be done from the keyboard. Tab moves through the columns' heading buttons, "+ Add" among them on the Backlog and Next sprint, the cards and rows, each list's "Add story" line after its last card, and the open story's fields in order, and the focused one shows the theme's focus ring. In a column, Up and Down move between its cards and rows, as J and K do, J down and K up, Down or J from the last reaching its "Add story" line, where Enter opens the composer, and Left and Right move focus to the column beside it, onto its card or row nearest the focused one's height, skipping a column with none, and scrolling the board sideways to show it; Enter opens or closes the focused story, A accepts the focused proposal, E edits it, and R rejects it. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the focused story up or down within its column, and Alt+Left and Alt+Right move it into the column beside it, as dragging does, where that column takes it: from the Backlog Alt+Right moves it to the end of Next sprint, and from Next sprint Alt+Left moves it to the end of the Backlog, focus going with it; anywhere else, as a story in Current sprint or Done, or toward a column that takes no drops, they do nothing. Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on macOS) opens the composer at the foot of the list that has focus, the Backlog or Next sprint, when focus is on its heading, one of its cards, or its "Add story" line, and at the foot of Next sprint otherwise, as when focus is in Current sprint, Done, Findings, the side panel, or nowhere on the board, scrolling the board sideways to show it. In a composer, Enter adds the story and opens the next composer beneath it, focus moving to its title, and Escape discards it, focus going back to what opened it, the "Add story" line or the heading's "+ Add". Ctrl+Enter moves the focused story to Next sprint, or to the Backlog from it, Ctrl+Shift+Enter sends the sprint, and M opens the focused story's mode picker, whose options are picked with the arrow keys and Enter. Delete deletes the focused story after a confirmation. X toggles the focused story in or out of the selection, as the parts say. While no card has focus, the shortcuts that act on one story, Enter, X, M, Ctrl+Enter, Delete, and the Alt arrows, act on the card under the pointer instead, as Linear's do, without moving focus to it, and do nothing when the pointer is over no card; with a card focused, they act on it, wherever the pointer is. None of them sends a story, as the sprint says. Escape closes what is open, a composer, a menu, the selection, cleared before anything else closes, then the side panel, whether it holds a story, drafting, or research, one at a time. Every button and control has a label a screen reader reads, as its visible text or, for an icon, its tooltip, and each shortcut is given in the tooltip of the control it stands for.
Ctrl+Shift+D opens Draft stories, focusing its request box, where Enter starts the draft, as Ctrl+Enter also does, and Shift+Enter adds a new line; Ctrl+Shift+R opens Research, focusing its box, where Ctrl+Enter starts it; F6 moves focus between the board's regions, the pause bar, each column, the bulk bar while it is shown, the side panel when it is open, and back, so each is a single stop; within the pause bar, Tab reaches Resume and Approve and send sprint; within the bulk bar, Tab reaches its buttons in order; within an open story, Tab reaches its steps, its Add step, and its Spec citations, each citation opening on Enter; and within Findings, Enter opens the focused finding in its tab. The Start automatically switch is reached by Tab on Next sprint's heading and toggled with Space. Focus never lands on something hidden or disabled, and after an action it stays where it was or moves to what the action made, as the new story or the next proposal, never back to the top.
