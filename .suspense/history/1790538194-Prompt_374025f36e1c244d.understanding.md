- [A Freeform task's header shows the prompt as sent, with no hidden anchor named](spec/scope/prompt-mode/modes/index.pi)
- [Freeform tasks are never sent to another mode, alone or in a batch](spec/scope/prompt-mode/modes/index.pi)
